For broadband operators navigating an increasingly competitive landscape, multi-dwelling units (MDUs) represent both a massive growth opportunity and a complex deployment headache. High density and underserved resident bases make MDUs an attractive target for subscriber acquisition, yet physical infrastructure limitations in brownfield properties frequently stall projects or inflate capital expenditures beyond viability.
