The Evolution of Fiber Broadband Networks As fixed-line fiber optic networks continue their rapid expansion, operators face a persistent challenge: achieving true interoperability between optical network terminals (ONTs) or optical network units (ONUs) and optical line terminals (OLTs). While passive optical network (PON) technologies have advanced significantly, the promise of seamless multi-vendor integration remains largely unfulfilled.
