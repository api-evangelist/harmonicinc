From Burden to Breakthrough Brownfield multi-dwelling unit ( MDUs) are often viewed as complex and costly to upgrade. In reality, many properties already have existing coax or fiber that can be leveraged, and residents in those buildings are demanding more bandwidth. With the right approach and technology, these legacy buildings can be transformed from perceived burdens into strategic assets that support new revenue and sustainable growth.
