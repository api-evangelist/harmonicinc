Improving quality of experience for each subscriber is the next frontier in subscriber satisfaction and retention. Unified DOCSIS 4.0 takes DOCSIS 4.0 to a new level, offering a revolutionary solution that empowers operators with access to both Full Duplex (FDX) and Frequency Division Duplex (FDD) DOCSIS 4.0 technologies. With virtualized solutions, operators can leverage the valuable combination of edge compute infrastructure with cloud services to adapt and perform for best quality of experience.
